    Why Asasly Arabic

    Most apps hand you words to memorise. Asasly builds the ground they stand on.

    A connected foundation

    Letters, sounds and patterns are taught as one system, so every new word lands on something you already understand.

    One clear path

    Levels move from the alphabet to reading and understanding, each lesson resting on the one before it.

    Made for non-Arabs

    Explanations in your own language, and Modern Standard Arabic as the door to the Quran, books and media.
